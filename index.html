2. Visiting this website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the page and keep its service secure. 3. Newsletter

If you sign up, we process your email address, your chosen language and a record of your consent (when you signed up and confirmed, which form you used, and your IP address). We use this to send you practical insights, book news and, later, offers for the book, roughly every two weeks. We only send the newsletter with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you confirm through a link in a confirmation email (double opt-in). You can unsubscribe at any time through the link in every email.

We do not track whether you open our emails. We do record which links you click, to see which topics are useful. This is part of your newsletter consent; unsubscribing stops it.

We use MailerLite (MailerLite Limited, Dublin, Ireland) to run the sign-up forms and send the newsletter. MailerLite acts on our behalf under a data processing agreement and stores subscriber data in the EU. The sign-up form is loaded from MailerLite when you open this page, so MailerLite receives your IP address even if you don’t sign up.

4. Emailing us

If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply. Our email runs on Microsoft 365, which processes it on our behalf. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract-related enquiries, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Who receives your data

Only the two of us and the service providers named above. We never sell your data or share it for other companies’ advertising. Some providers may access data from outside the EU; such transfers are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 14 days. Subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe. After that, we keep your email address on a block list so you are not added again, and your consent record for up to three years so we can prove you consented. Emails are deleted when no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its processing and to receive it in a portable format. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Netherlands) or the LDI NRW (Germany).

8. Changes
